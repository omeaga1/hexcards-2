Hex Cards Source on GitHub

Builds from real ranked games, sent straight to your client.

Hex Cards is a free League of Legends companion for Windows. It gives every champion 1 to 3 builds from games at your rank, lights up the item swaps that matter against the enemy team in champ select, and imports runes, item sets and spells into the League client.

What it does

Builds by rank

Builds, tier lists and roles are worked out separately for Iron to Silver, Gold to Emerald, and Diamond and above, so what you see fits the games you play.

Swaps for this game

Each build lists the items players buy more, and win with, against specific team comps: anti-heal against healers, magic resist against AP-heavy teams. In champ select they light up for the enemy picks you see.

Imports that just work

One click sets your rune page, adds item sets for every build and picks your spells, with Flash on D or F. Hex Cards only edits the pages and sets it created and never deletes yours.

Where the numbers come from

Ranked solo games on the current patch
Players are drawn from Riot's ranked ladders in North America, Europe West and Korea, across three rank brackets. Each game's match data and timeline give the runes, item purchases with timing, skill order and result.
Grouped into real builds
Games are grouped by the kind of items bought, so a champion's Bruiser and Lethality builds each get their own runes and skills from the same games. Every rune page is checked against the current patch before it's published.
Refreshed through the patch
A scheduled job keeps collecting and republishes builds every 6 hours.
Only statistics are published
Builds, tier lists and roles are aggregates. Raw match data is encrypted and kept for processing only. No player names, Riot IDs or match histories are stored or shown.
Only what the client already shows you
The app reads your own champ select from the League client on your PC: your pick, your role and the champions visible on both teams. It doesn't reveal hidden names, give in-game timers or automate anything.

Run it from source

Needs Node.js, Rust and the Microsoft C++ Build Tools on Windows.

git clone https://github.com/omeaga1/hexcards-2.git
cd hexcards-2
npm install
npm run app
